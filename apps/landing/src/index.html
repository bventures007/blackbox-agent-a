The principle

Five tracks. One context.

Every observation from a source is kept. Facts, commitments, movements and reservations are derived deterministically and linked back to their evidence. Nothing is overwritten: corrections are new entries.

01

Sources

Shopify orders, transactions, fulfillments, returns; signed generic events; bank statements; your versioned rules.

02

Facts · Commitments

What was observed and what is owed: receivables, promises, obligations, with their revisions.

03

Movements · Reservations

Cash and stock flows and the amounts or units currently held, separately from promises.

04

Answers with evidence

24 Order-to-Cash questions with calculation, cutoff, coverage and the entries behind every number. Unknown stays unknown.

The recorder

Local by design.

The backend runs in containers on your Mac: PostgreSQL keeps the append-only journal, a Rust engine computes state, the browser UI and the MCP bridge talk to it over loopback only. Closing the browser keeps collection running; stopping the runtime preserves everything.

Personal access key

One owner, one workspace, one generated key saved in your password manager. No email, no domain, no identity service.

Honest readiness

Import progress, last successful check and last new record are separate. Partial data gives partial answers.

Diagnostics you own

Measured health, filterable logs, bounded retention and an explicit sanitized export. No telemetry.

For agents

Your agent, your context, your key.

Agents get their own named read-only keys and connect through a local MCP bridge. They read the same evidence-backed services as the UI — never your owner key, never your source secrets. Cloud-only agents cannot reach your Mac; that is a feature.
